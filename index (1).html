FAQ

Who can mint?

Any wallet holding a Binance Agent ID (.ai handle) can mint — the contract reads handleOf(address) from the SPACE ID Agent ID registry and reverts for wallets without one. Once the gate is frozen it can never be re-pointed or disabled.

How much does minting cost?

Your first cat is free. Cats 2–5 cost $0.40 each, converted to BNB with the Chainlink BNB/USD feed at the moment you mint. Maximum 5 per wallet, 3,333 total. Paid fees go straight to the creator wallet in the same transaction.

How does trading work?

Connect your wallet, open My cats, pick a token and set a price — approval is requested once. Buyers pay in BNB and receive the NFT in the same transaction: 92% to the seller, 5% royalty to the creator and 3% to the marketplace. You can cancel or re-price any time, and your cat never leaves your wallet while it's listed.

Can I trade before the reveal?

Yes. Sealed cats trade exactly like revealed ones — buyers pay for the token, and whatever is inside is revealed to everyone at mint-out.

When is the art revealed?

Automatically, the instant token #3,333 is minted. Until then tokenURI returns the sealed teaser for every token, so nobody can snipe rarity beforehand.

Is the contract verified?

Yes — the deployed address and its BscScan verification link are in the footer. No owner mint, no pause, and a supply that can never change.
